Computer Vision Crowd Estimation Methodology & Technical Limitations

People counting is calculated using neural bounding box extraction combined with Euclidean centroid tracking across consecutive video frames (with a 20-frame maximum disappearance memory). Crowd density heatmaps are rendered using 2D Gaussian point accumulation mapped through a 256-step thermal gradient lookup table.

Operational Limitations: Severe target occlusion in ultra-dense crowds (>3 persons/m²) may induce temporary track fragmentation or momentary undercounting. Camera tilt angles steeper than 45° diminish facial feature visibility. Lighting variations below 20 lux increase tracking variance.

Explainable Risk Prioritization & Severity Scoring Rules

Alerts are prioritized strictly according to transparent, explainable operational security criteria rather than unverified predictive heuristics:

CRITICAL (Score 90–100)

Immediate danger: Physical perimeter intrusion in restricted zones or detected physical collapse / falls.

HIGH (Score 70–89)

Escalated hazard: Sustained zone overcrowding (≥100% capacity) or protracted suspicious loitering.

MEDIUM (Score 40–69)

Compliance policy violations: Missing ID badge in secured sector or unmasked cluster in transit corridors.

LOW (Score 10–39)

Informational: Brief transient crowd spikes or unusual velocity deviations without perimeter breach.

False-Positive & False-Negative Analysis (Academic Disclosure)
Mask Detection Limitations:
  • High-collar jackets and thick dark winter scarves occasionally induce False Positives (2.6%).
  • Flesh-toned or ornate fabric masks blend with natural cheek skin texture inducing False Negatives (2.6%).
  • Improper mask wearing (nose exposed) is intentionally classified as unmasked in compliance mode.
ID Card & Anomaly Tracking Bounds:
  • Patterned shirts with vivid royal-blue vertical graphics can cause momentary ID-lanyard confusion.
  • Subjects bending down to pick up items on the ground can register transient Fall candidate events (resolved by 3-frame temporal confirmation).
  • Crowd densities exceeding 3.5 persons/m² induce optical occlusion where tracking switches occur.
